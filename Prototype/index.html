📏 How to count physically (show / hide)

Choose one of twelve aiming directions from the on-map compass. Distance advances along that hex axis. After rolling, choose LEFT or RIGHT and HOW MANY Control steps to use. Wind moves the ball first at full strength. Then all available positive Control can be spent on wind-axis movement, backspin, or topspin; Control never automatically cancels wind. For easy physical counting, take your Distance first, wind second, then spend Control to shift sideways from the wind-adjusted hex. With zero wind, Control begins at the original landing hex. You can also spend Control to reduce distance (backspin) or increase distance (topspin), one hex per point. Lateral Control moves the ball after wind and does not replace forward distance steps. Wind is applied before lateral Control unless Wind Shield is activated. Wind starts at the unadjusted landing hex and can push in any of the six directions, including backward toward the tee. Some clubs have automatic club abilities. Tap-In: once per hole, automatically sink a putt from 1 hex away. Putting: tap in from 1 hex for +1 stroke; from 2+ hexes, roll 1–4 to move that many hexes closer or 5–6 for an instant hole. Discard abilities for +1 hex each after rolls of 1–4. All clubs now show three distance tiers (1 / 2–4 / 5–6), with the former roll-6 maximum as the new long value. A natural Distance 6 grants +1 Control. All clubs fly over trees without obstruction. Trees prevent landing, but never block a shot in flight. High Launch is currently redundant pending a future flight-path obstacle. The Fallen Trees course hazard places additional trees on fairway hexes for the entire hole. Three sand-trap clusters occupy the middle of the fairway. Draw clubs equal to the current hole Par; the first hole and each club-deck reshuffle guarantee your signature driver. The tabletop rule is Par 3: 3 clubs, Par 4: 4 clubs, Par 5: 5 clubs. Draw three random ability cards from a 14-card deck; pre-shot cards alter club selection before rolling, and post-roll cards alter the shot. Play at most one per shot. Played ability cards are not replaced until the next hole. The pink ring shows the projected wind-adjusted landing hex. Each hole begins with one course hazard check; an activated event affects the whole hole. Shots can fly over water; water only penalizes the final wind-adjusted landing hex (+1 stroke and nearest legal-hex relocation). Wind always resolves before checking for a hole-out. A shot landing on the cup can be blown away; wind can also carry the ball into the cup! Each golfer has a unique six-face Control Die and a powerful once-per-round dice-manipulation Character Special Ability. This prevents Control from secretly increasing range.
